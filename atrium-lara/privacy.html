Atrium Lara privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Atrium Lara is a personal app used only by its developer, Johnny Nguyen, on his own Mac.

What it accesses

With his consent through Google sign-in, the app reads, drafts and sends email in his own Gmail account (scope gmail.modify) and reads his email address to show which account is connected.

How the data is used

Email is fetched on his Mac only when he asks the assistant about it. Short summaries or excerpts are passed to the voice service (ElevenLabs) during that conversation so the assistant can answer; ElevenLabs keeps conversations for 30 days. Nothing is sent from his account without his approval on screen. Sign-in tokens stay in the macOS keychain.

No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models. Atrium Lara's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by signing out of Gmail in Atrium's Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
